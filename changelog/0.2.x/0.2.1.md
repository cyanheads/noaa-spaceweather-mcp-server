# 0.2.1 — 2026-09-22

## Added

- **`get_aurora_forecast` daylight gate:** `sunElevationDeg` and `darkness` (`day`/`civil_twilight`/`nautical_twilight`/`dark`) at the requested coordinates and forecast time. In daylight the verdict reports aurora as not visible regardless of the model probability; in twilight it adds that only bright aurora will show. ([#36](https://github.com/cyanheads/noaa-spaceweather-mcp-server/issues/36))
- **`get_aurora_forecast` horizon view:** `horizonMaxPercent`, `horizonMaxLatitude`, `horizonDistanceKm` — the strongest reading within 1000 km poleward and ±2° longitude of the requested point. When it reaches 10% and beats the overhead reading, the verdict notes aurora may be visible low on the horizon. ([#37](https://github.com/cyanheads/noaa-spaceweather-mcp-server/issues/37))

## Changed

- **`get_aurora_forecast` `feed_moved`** now also fires, in one attempt, when a coordinate lookup finds no parseable `Forecast Time` — the daylight gate has no instant to evaluate without it. ([#36](https://github.com/cyanheads/noaa-spaceweather-mcp-server/issues/36))
